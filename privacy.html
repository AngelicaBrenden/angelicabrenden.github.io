Privacy Policy

StudyScan · Last updated 1 September 2026

In short: your scanned pages, notes and highlights stay on your device. StudyScan has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and I never see your study material.

Who I am

StudyScan is made by Angelica Brenden, an independent app developer in Norway. If you have any questions about this policy, email Angelicabrenden@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in StudyScan is stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's own storage:

  • The page images you scan
  • The text recognised from those pages
  • Your highlights, notes and reading position
  • The summaries the app has made

None of this is uploaded anywhere. It is included in your device backup if you back up your device, and it is deleted when you delete a book or remove the app.

Text recognition and summaries

Reading the text from your scanned pages happens entirely on your device, using Apple's built-in text recognition. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.

Summaries are also made on your device, by Apple Intelligence, on devices that support it. Your pages do not leave the device.

This means your study material is never sent to me, to any server of mine, or to any other company, for either text recognition or summaries.

Advertising

StudyScan uses Google AdMob to show an optional rewarded ad when you choose to unlock a summary for free instead of buying StudyScan Pro.

The app requests non-personalised ads only. That means ads are not selected based on a profile of you, and StudyScan does not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites. To deliver an ad and prevent fraud, Google still receives technical information such as device type, coarse region and whether an ad was shown or completed. Google's own handling of that data is described in Google Privacy & Terms.

Buying StudyScan Pro removes the ads entirely.

Purchases

StudyScan Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. Payment is processed by Apple, and I never see your card details or your Apple Account information. The app only records, on your device, whether the purchase has been made.

What I do not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-up
  • No analytics or usage tracking of my own
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No access to your photo library, contacts or location

The camera is used only while you are scanning pages, and the images go straight into the app's own storage.

Children

StudyScan is made for students and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete a book in the app to remove its pages, notes and summaries from your device.
  • Buy StudyScan Pro to remove ads entirely.
  • Delete the app to remove everything StudyScan has stored.

Changes to this policy

If StudyScan changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and change the date at the top.
