Privacy Policy

Traveltips · Last updated 6 October 2026

In short: Traveltips has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. What you save, pin and plan stays on your own iPhone. The only things that leave it are the tips and routes you choose to share — and those are meant to be public, so think of them as something you are publishing rather than something you are storing.

Who I am

Traveltips is made by Angelica Brenden, an independent app developer in Norway. If you have any questions about this policy, or you want something removed, email Angelicabrenden@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

These never leave your device, and I cannot see them:

  • Tips you have saved with the bookmark
  • Trips you have made, with their names and dates
  • Places you have pinned on a trip, and the notes you wrote on them — unless you publish that trip as a travel route
  • The destinations you have looked at, and anything you have searched for
  • People you have blocked, and tips you have reported and hidden
  • Your nickname, and whether you prefer light or dark mode

All of it is stored inside the app on your phone. Delete the app and it is gone.

What becomes public when you share a tip

Sharing a tip is publishing. When you add one, this is sent to the app's shared database and becomes readable by everyone using Traveltips:

  • The title and text you wrote
  • The category you chose
  • The place it belongs to, and the point on the map if you set one
  • Any photos you attached
  • The nickname you chose

Use a first name or something made up as your nickname. It is shown on every tip you share, and changing it later only affects new tips — the ones already published keep the name they went out with.

Nothing is visible to anyone else until I have read it and approved it. Until then only you can see it, marked as waiting.

What becomes public when you publish a travel route

A trip is private until you publish it. If you do, the name, your description, the dates, and every pinned place with its note and its position become public, under your nickname. The same approval step applies: I read the whole route before it goes out.

You can take a published route back down at any time, from the trip itself. It stays on your phone either way.

How many people saved a tip

When you bookmark a tip, that is recorded so the person who wrote it can see how many people found it useful. They are only ever shown a number. Neither they nor I can see who saved what.

Where the shared content is kept

Shared tips and routes live in Apple's CloudKit, in a database belonging to this app. There is no server of my own and no account to create: the app identifies you through an anonymous identifier that Apple generates for this app alone. It is not your Apple Account, not your email address and not your name, and it cannot be used to recognise you in any other app.

That identifier is what makes it possible to show you your own tips, let you delete them, and keep one person from reporting the same tip over and over. Apple's own privacy policy covers how they handle the data they store on my behalf.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location for two things: to show where you are on the map, and to list tips near you. It is used while you have the app open, it is accurate to about a kilometre, and it is never sent anywhere or written down. Say no and the rest of the app works exactly as before.

Notifications

Notifications exist only so that I can be told when a tip is waiting for approval. The app does not send you marketing, and nothing is pushed to ordinary users.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or usage statistics
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party software collecting anything in the background
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No profile of you, anywhere

The maps come from Apple Maps, which is part of iOS. The photos of destinations are from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, and the photographer and licence for each one are named in the app under Settings → Photo credits.

Removing what you have shared

You are in control of your own content:

  • A single tip can be deleted from the tip itself
  • Settings → Delete all my tips removes everything you have shared, for everyone, in one go
  • A published route can be taken down from the trip

If something is already gone from the app but you believe a copy is still out there, email me and I will remove it.

Reporting and blocking

Every shared tip and route can be reported, and every contributor can be blocked. Reporting hides it from you straight away. Blocking is yours alone, stays on your phone, and the other person is never told. I read what is reported and remove anything that should not be there.

Children

Traveltips is not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has shared something through the app, email me and I will remove it.

Changes

If what the app stores changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
